# A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks

Edgar F. Codd, *Communications of the ACM* 13(6):377–387, June 1970.
doi:10.1145/362384.362685

The founding paper of the [[relational-model]]. A theoretical proposal many doubted
could be implemented efficiently; RDBMS/SQL nonetheless dominated by the mid-1980s.
DDIA Ch 2 footnote: Codd's original model already allowed **"nonsimple domains"** —
nested relations as values, i.e. JSON/XML-like nesting 30+ years before SQL added it.
