# The Log-Structured Merge-Tree (LSM-Tree)

Patrick O'Neil, Edward Cheng, Dieter Gawlick, Elizabeth O'Neil. *Acta Informatica*
33(4):351–385, June 1996.

The paper that named and described the LSM-tree, building on log-structured
filesystem work (Rosenblum & Ousterhout 1992). The basis for the
[[sstables-and-lsm-trees|SSTable/LSM]] engine family (the term *SSTable/memtable*
itself comes from the later [[bigtable-paper]]).
