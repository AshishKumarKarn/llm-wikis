# Impossibility of Distributed Consensus with One Faulty Process

Michael J. Fischer, Nancy Lynch, Michael S. Paterson, *JACM* 32(2):374–382, April
1985.

The **FLP result**: no *deterministic* algorithm can always reach
[[consensus]] if a node may crash — *in the asynchronous model* (no clocks/timeouts).
Of great theoretical importance, but consensus is solvable in practice once timeouts
or randomness are allowed. *(DDIA Ch 9)*
