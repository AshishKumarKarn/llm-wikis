# What Goes Around Comes Around

Michael Stonebraker & Joseph M. Hellerstein, in *Readings in Database Systems*, 4th
ed., MIT Press, pp. 2–41, 2005.

DDIA Ch 2's source for the historical argument that the document-vs-relational debate
**re-runs the 1970s** hierarchical (IMS) vs. network (CODASYL) vs. relational "great
debate" — and that each anti-relational wave (network, object, XML, NoSQL) generated
hype that never lasted.
