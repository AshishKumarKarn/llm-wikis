# EM — People Management

Interview answers for people management, team leadership, cross-functional conflict,
and performance management (see [[sources/leadership-study-guide]]).

## Performance & Career Development

**Q: How do you manage performance and career growth?**
- Quarterly goal-setting aligned to org OKRs.
- Clear growth paths defined (IC vs Tech Lead vs Manager).
- Bi-weekly 1:1s focused on growth, not just delivery.
- Stretch assignments aligned with team priorities.
- Continuous feedback model — no annual surprises.
- Measurement: skill improvement, impact delivered, ownership growth.

**Q: How do you handle an underperforming engineer?**
1. 1:1 to understand root cause (workload, clarity, personal issues).
2. Break tasks into smaller milestones with explicit deadlines.
3. Daily/weekly check-ins to track progress.
4. Pair with a peer for accountability and support.
5. Document missed commitments; if pattern continues → formal PIP.

**Q: How do you balance a high performer and an average performer?**
- **High performer:** stretch assignments, visibility in leadership forums, recognition (spot awards, public praise).
- **Average performer:** skill-building opportunities, mentorship, achievable goals.
- Rotate responsibilities so both contribute meaningfully. Recognition should reflect output, collaboration, and reliability.

**Q: How do you handle misalignment between growth and team needs?**
"If exploration doesn't align with roadmap, have a transparent conversation. Convert it into a
time-bound innovation spike — growth preserved, delivery protected."

## Cross-Team Conflicts

**Q: How do you resolve cross-team priority conflicts?**
1. Start with direct EM-to-EM discussion.
2. Focus conversation on company-level goals, not team-level ownership.
3. Quantify impact — what revenue, OKRs, or customer commitments are affected.
4. Explore phased or parallel delivery options.
5. Document trade-offs before escalation.

**Q: How do you escalate effectively?**
- Escalate with: clear problem statement + data-backed impact + options with pros/cons.
- Use RACI/DACI decision frameworks.
- "Escalation is not failure — **late escalation is**."

**Q: What if neither team can compromise?**
1. Can scope be reduced?
2. Can we temporarily share resources (SWAT team)?
3. Can delivery be phased?
If none viable → escalate early with timeline comparison, revenue/OKR impact, capacity constraints,
and 2–3 decision options. Ask for a clear priority call.

## Technical Talent Management

**Q: How do you handle a senior engineer who constantly challenges your architectural decisions?**
1. Acknowledge their expertise publicly.
2. Redirect to data-driven discussion (benchmarks, scalability metrics, trade-offs).
3. Facilitate a design review session outside the meeting.
4. Document decisions and rationale.
5. If conflict persists → "disagree and commit" — align on delivery, revisit after iteration.

**Q: How do you grow engineers technically?**
- IC vs leadership track mapping early.
- Stretch projects aligned with team goals.
- Architecture review involvement for senior ICs.
- Knowledge forums / internal tech wiki.
- Mentee assignments for tech leads (minimum 2/year).

## Goals and Performance Review Framework

### Goal dimensions
| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| On-time delivery | >95% milestones met |
| Code/delivery quality | >95% (< defect rate in reviewed modules) |
| Team collaboration | >90% standup attendance |
| Team enablement | ≥1 facilitation/quarter |
| New initiatives/enhancements | >2/year |
| Compliance | 100% |
| Learning (20h/year) | 100% |

### Rating levels
- **Needs Support** — inconsistent results; needs guidance; doesn't demonstrate CARE values.
- **Value Performer** — meets expectations; learns and grows; delivers reliably.
- **Trailblazer** — consistently exceeds expectations; inspires others; operates at next level.

### Review process
- Reportees self-assess percentage achieved per goal.
- Manager assesses based on observation + facts.
- Every 1:1 recorded in system; discuss good + improvement areas.
