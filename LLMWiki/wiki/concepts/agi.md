# AGI — Artificial General Intelligence

---

## Definition

An AI system capable of performing any cognitive task a human can perform, across arbitrary domains, at human level or above. Distinguished from narrow AI (e.g., image recognition, chess) which excels only at specific tasks.

There is no universally agreed definition. [[entities/openbrain]] in [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]] declares AGI achieved when it releases Agent-3-mini in July 2027 — framed as marketing as much as technical milestone.

## Why "AGI" is slippery

Agent-3 in the scenario has a "very different skill profile" from humans: knows more facts than any human, handles any programming language, solves well-specified coding problems extremely quickly — but is bad at simple long-horizon tasks, like beating video games it hasn't played before. It is "like a scatterbrained employee who thrives under careful management."

The moment of "AGI" is less a sharp threshold and more a moving target: for each domain automated, humans redefine AGI as the next domain not yet mastered.

---

## Relationship to the intelligence explosion

Once an AI system is capable enough to significantly accelerate AI R&D, the transition from "near-AGI" to "ASI" may be very fast. In [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]], this gap is about 5 months (August → December 2027). This is the [[concepts/intelligence-explosion]] playing out.
