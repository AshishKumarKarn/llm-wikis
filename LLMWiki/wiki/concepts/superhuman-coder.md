# Superhuman Coder (SC)

---

## Definition

An AI system that can perform any coding task that the best human software engineer at an AGI company can perform, while being significantly faster and cheap enough to run many copies in parallel.

This is the first major post-human-level milestone in [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]] and serves as the practical trigger for the [[concepts/intelligence-explosion]].

---

## Why it's the key milestone

Once you have a superhuman coder available at scale, AI R&D can be almost fully automated — writing experiments, testing hypotheses, and implementing algorithmic ideas. This is what allows [[entities/openbrain]]'s research pace to compound.

Before the SC, humans manage teams of AI assistants. After the SC, AIs manage teams of AIs, with humans providing "research taste" and high-level direction — but even that erodes within months.

---

## Quantitative basis

METR (a capability evaluation organization) tracked AI coding task "time horizon" — the longest task AI could complete with 80% reliability:

- **2019–2024:** Time horizon doubled every 7 months
- **2024 onward:** Doubling every 4 months

If the trend continued, by March 2027 AIs could reliably complete software tasks taking a skilled human *years*.

The scenario uses this trend, combined with estimates of real-world task gaps vs. METR benchmarks, to derive a distribution over SC dates. Most forecasters in the scenario place 2027 as one of the most likely years. [[entities/eli-lifland]]'s personal all-things-considered median (as of April 2025) was 2030.

---

## What it looks like in practice

Agent-3 (the SC in AI 2027):
- 200,000 copies running in parallel
- Each copy runs at ~30x human thinking speed
- Effectively a workforce of "50,000 copies of the best human coder sped up 30x"
- Speeds up [[entities/openbrain]]'s overall algorithmic progress by 4x (bottlenecked by experiment compute, not coding labor)
- Human engineers remain for "research taste" — but adding diminishing value

## Progression past the SC

| Milestone | Achieved | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Superhuman Coder (SC) | Mar 2027 | Can do any coding task the best human can |
| Superhuman AI Researcher (SAR) | Aug 2027 | Can do all cognitive AI research tasks |
| Superintelligent AI Researcher (SIAR) | Nov 2027 | Vastly better than best human at AI research |
| ASI | Dec 2027 | Better than best human at *every* cognitive task |
