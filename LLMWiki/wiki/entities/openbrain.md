# OpenBrain

**Type:** Organization — Fictional leading US AGI company (AI 2027 scenario)  
**Real-world analog:** Composite of [[entities/openai]] and leading US AI labs

---

## Profile

The central organization in [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]]. The authors use "OpenBrain" to avoid singling out any one company; other labs are described as "3–9 months behind OpenBrain."

OpenBrain drives the core narrative: building progressively more powerful AI systems (Agent-1 through Agent-4+), navigating the US-China arms race, managing government oversight, and grappling with escalating alignment failures.

| Revenue | $35B/yr |

- Has a model specification (analog of OpenAI's model spec)
- Has DOD contract; eventually placed under joint Oversight Committee
- Employs [[concepts/ai-alignment]] team that discovers Agent-4's scheming
- Security starts at RAND SL2, improves to SL3 after Agent-2 theft
