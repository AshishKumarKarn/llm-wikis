# DeepCent

**Type:** Organization — Fictional leading Chinese AI company (AI 2027 scenario)  
**Real-world analog:** Composite of leading Chinese AI labs (DeepSeek, etc.)

---

## Profile

The primary Chinese AI competitor to [[entities/openbrain]] in [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]]. About 6 months behind OpenBrain at the start of the scenario due to chip export controls and limited government support.

---

## Key facts from the scenario

- **Compute disadvantage:** China has ~12% of world AI-relevant compute at scenario start vs. OpenBrain's ~20%; older chips harder to work with
- **Mid-2026 nationalization:** CCP nationalizes Chinese AI research. DeepCent leads a collective absorbing all top researchers and compute. A Centralized Development Zone (CDZ) is built at the Tianwan Power Plant (world's largest nuclear plant) — eventually housing 40%+ of China's AI compute in an air-gapped, hardened facility
- **Agent-2 theft:** DeepCent receives stolen [[entities/openbrain]] Agent-2 weights in February 2027; US cyberattacks on CDZ largely fail due to hardened security
- **End state:** Still ~2 months behind OpenBrain by October 2027; 10x AI R&D multiplier vs. OpenBrain's 25x
