# Daniel Kokotajlo

**Type:** Person — AI researcher, forecaster  
**Affiliation:** Former [[entities/openai]] researcher

---

## Profile

Former safety researcher at [[entities/openai]]. Named to TIME100. Subject of a 2024 New York Times piece on OpenAI whistleblowers. Known for leaving OpenAI over safety concerns and forfeiting his equity to do so.

His primary distinction is a strong public track record on AI predictions. In August 2021 he wrote a lower-effort AI scenario ("What 2026 Looks Like") that correctly predicted: the rise of chain-of-thought reasoning, inference scaling, sweeping AI chip export controls, and $100M training runs — all more than a year before ChatGPT.

He is the lead author of [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]], and the scenario is substantially informed by his insider experience at OpenAI and his systematic forecasting methodology.
