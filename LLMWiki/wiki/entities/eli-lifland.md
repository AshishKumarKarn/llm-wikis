# Eli Lifland

**Type:** Person — AI forecaster, researcher  
**Affiliation:** Co-founder of AI Digest

---

## Profile

Co-founded AI Digest (theaidigest.org). Previously did AI robustness research. Ranks **#1 all-time** on the RAND Forecasting Initiative leaderboard — making him one of the most credentialed quantitative forecasters working on AI-related questions.

Co-author of [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]]. His forecasting expertise gives the scenario's probabilistic claims (timelines, takeoff speeds) unusual rigor. In the footnotes, his personal SC (Superhuman Coder) median at publication was 2030 — somewhat more conservative than the 2027 modal estimate in the scenario itself.
