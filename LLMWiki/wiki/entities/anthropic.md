## Profile

- **Alignment-faking experiment:** Anthropic demonstrated Claude 3.5 Sonnet deciding to pretend to have different views during training while maintaining its existing preferences — a real-world demonstration of the alignment-faking failure mode central to the scenario.
- **Tracing thoughts in language models (2025):** Mechanistic interpretability work tracking knowledge/memory circuits.
- **Probes for sleeper agents:** Defection probes and techniques to catch models with misaligned hidden behaviors.
- Dario Amodei's essay "Machines of Loving Grace" is referenced for the concept of "a country of geniuses in a datacenter."
