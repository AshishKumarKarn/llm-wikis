# OpenAI

**Type:** Organization — US AI lab  
**CEO:** Sam Altman  
**Real-world analog:** [[entities/openbrain]] (in AI 2027 scenario)

---

## Profile

Leading US AI company; creator of the GPT series, ChatGPT, and Operator. Has publicly predicted AGI within 5 years (Sam Altman). Has published a model specification describing intended AI behavior.

In [[sources/2026-05-15-ai-2027]], the fictional [[entities/openbrain]] is explicitly stated to represent a composite of OpenAI and the leading US AGI lab more generally. Key real-world facts cited in the scenario: GPT-4 trained at ~$2×10^{25}$ FLOP; model spec exists at model-spec.openai.com; has a government contract with DOD.

[[entities/daniel-kokotajlo]] is a former OpenAI researcher who left over safety concerns.
